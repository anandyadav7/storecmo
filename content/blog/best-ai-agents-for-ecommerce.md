If you run a lean ecommerce team, the useful question is not "which AI agent is best" but "which one matches the bottleneck that is actually costing me money this month". Short answer: pick Gorgias AI Agent when support volume is the problem, Rep AI when on-site conversion is, Klaviyo AI when retention messaging is, Triple Whale Moby when measurement is, and Shopify Sidekick as the free baseline inside the admin. StoreCMO, our own product, is being built for the layer none of those cover: deciding what matters this month. Below is the framework we used to compare them, scored on store context, analysis depth, execution, and workflow coherence.

## What actually separates an ecommerce AI agent from a chatbot?

The five agents compared here are Gorgias AI Agent (an AI Agent built for ecommerce that learns your brand, your customers, and your workflows, and pulls real-time data from platforms like Shopify), Rep AI (an Agentic Commerce Operating System that monitors hundreds of behavioral signals across your store), Klaviyo AI (K:AI agents that create, resolve, and optimize), Triple Whale Moby 2 (an AI teammate built specifically for ecommerce that, in Triple Whale's words, already knows your business), and Shopify Sidekick (which Shopify describes as your AI Shopify expert, with direct access to your store's data). StoreCMO is our own product, so read every mention of it below as our positioning rather than an evidenced comparison.

Two criteria do most of the sorting. **Store context**: does the agent work from your catalog, your customers and your performance data? **Execution**: does it act, or only recommend?

## Which agent is right for your bottleneck?

**Support volume: Gorgias AI Agent.** It automatically handles order tracking, returns and FAQs, offers discounts and upsells based on live inventory and shopper data, and lets you view the reasoning behind every AI response so you can correct it with a thumbs up or a guidance tweak. Best when tickets are eating your week.

**On-site conversion: Rep AI.** Its homepage lists the AI Sales Agent as starting at $99/month, while its pricing page lists a Launch tier at $299/month with a per-visitor component, so confirm the figure that applies to your store's sessions and catalogue size before you plan around it.

**Retention messaging: Klaviyo AI.** Composer analyses your performance across campaigns, segments and flows to find opportunities with the greatest revenue potential, then builds the campaign for you. Klaviyo says K:AI is trained on your brand, website, catalog and profiles, so the quality of that data sets the ceiling.

**Measurement: Triple Whale Moby 2.** Use it when you cannot tell which channel is working; it manages ad campaigns across Meta, Google and TikTok and forecasts next month's order volume for every product SKU on top of connected business data.

**Nothing broken yet: Shopify Sidekick.** Shopify's own page is direct: "Sidekick is included with your Shopify plan. Features and usage limits vary by plan." It is the free baseline to set up before paying for anything.

## Why does store context decide how useful an agent is?

Context is the difference between an answer about ecommerce and an answer about your store. Four of the five vendors describe their context model on their own product pages, and those descriptions tell you what each agent can actually reason about.

[Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/ai-agent) grounds its agent in brand-specific Guidance plus real-time data from platforms like Shopify: storefront details, order history, product catalogs, inventory levels and customer tags. [Klaviyo](https://www.klaviyo.com/solutions/ai) says K:AI is pre-trained on your data, policies and brand voice, and that conversations do not just get resolved but enrich your customer profiles, making every future campaign, follow-up and flow more personalized. [Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/) calls its layer a Context Engine and argues that generic AI tools fail because they lack complete business context and measurement validation. [Rep AI](https://www.hellorep.ai/) builds context from behaviour instead, monitoring hundreds of behavioral signals across your store.

Our view: test context retention before you buy. Ask the agent something only your store's data can answer, then ask a follow-up in a new session. If the answer resets, you have a copy tool with a login.

## Where does the stack leave a gap for lean teams?

Line up what the five agents actually do and the shape of the stack becomes obvious. Ticket resolution, on-site chat, lifecycle sends and ad activation all cluster at the doing end. Attribution, dashboards and forecasting cluster at the measuring end. Triple Whale puts the two together explicitly, describing a business intelligence layer that turns raw data into clarity through dashboards, reports and visualisations, and an activation layer that moves from measurement to activation.

## How should a two-person team actually roll one out?

A workable sequence, in order:

1. **Name the bottleneck in one sentence.** "We lose two mornings a week to order-status tickets" is a bottleneck. "We should use more AI" is not.
2. **Set up the native layer first.** Shopify Sidekick is included with your plan, and Shopify Flow is free on every Shopify plan. Set both up before paying for anything, and treat them as the bar a paid agent has to clear.
3. **Add exactly one paid agent, matched to that sentence.** Rep AI advertises a 30-day free trial with no credit card required and a six-click integration where its team handles the entire setup; Gorgias's cheapest plan is $40/month (Helpdesk at $10 plus AI Agent at $30), with 50 tickets and 30 automated interactions included. One agent per job beats one tool claiming every job.
4. **Keep consequential actions behind approval.** Refund limits, spend changes and live storefront edits should wait for a human. Klaviyo provides full audit trails, built-in simulations and guardrails for exactly this reason; use them.
5. **Model usage-based pricing before you scale.** Support agents typically combine a platform fee with per-resolution pricing, so a successful rollout makes the bill grow.

Give it two weeks of supervised runtime before you widen the guardrails.

## Where does this recommendation fail?

Three ways this recommendation fails, and they are worth knowing before you sign anything.

**It fails if you treat vendor figures as your baseline.** The pick-by-bottleneck logic above leans on what each vendor publishes. Rep AI publishes a 10–30% lift in CVR and a 50–70% reduction in support tickets; Triple Whale publishes a 42% increase in new customer revenue within 90 days. Neither is a guarantee for your store. If you size the decision on those numbers rather than on your own two-week test, the recommendation breaks.

**It fails on thin data.** Recommending Klaviyo AI for retention assumes Klaviyo is already trained on a clean catalog and clear policies, and recommending Triple Whale for measurement assumes the sources are connected; Triple Whale itself argues that AI without complete business context and measurement validation does not deliver. An agent grounded in an incomplete catalog will confidently produce plausible nonsense.

**It fails when volume grows.** Recommending Gorgias at $40/month ignores what happens next: past the included 50 tickets and 30 automated interactions, Gorgias charges $0.40 per ticket and $1.50 per automated interaction, and Rep AI's plans scale with site sessions and catalogue size. Success raises the bill. Model the cost at three times your current volume before committing.

And none of these picks remove human review. Someone still has to read what the agent produced and decide whether it matches how you actually want to run the store.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an AI agent for ecommerce?

An AI agent for ecommerce is software that works from your store's own data and takes actions in your systems, rather than only answering questions or drafting text. Gorgias AI Agent issuing a refund, and Klaviyo Composer building the audience, drafting the content and planning the send, both qualify. A tool that only writes copy does not.

### Can one AI agent replace a marketer?

No, and the credible vendors do not claim it. Klaviyo describes humans in the loop, and Gorgias shows the reasoning behind each response so a person can correct it. The realistic gain is that one person covers more ground, not that nobody covers it.

### Which agent should a store under $1M start with?

Start with the native layer: Sidekick is included with your Shopify plan, with features and usage limits varying by plan. Add Gorgias at $40/month once support volume genuinely hurts, or Rep AI if on-site conversion is the problem, after confirming its current pricing for your store size. Hold off on paid measurement tools until ad spend is large enough that a wrong call costs more than the subscription.
